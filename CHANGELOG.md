# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- **Your own goal.** Choose a line in the Journey panel (an HM, a key item, a Pokédex gift, a side trip, a legendary) and press **Guide me here**: the arrow leads there and the Next step panel shows it, with the story's next step under it. It lets go by itself when you get it, or with **Stop guiding**.
- Every HM, key item and Oak's aide gift now has its place on the map.

## 0.2.0

- An arrow at the border of the game screen points the way to the current objective, with the number of steps. It follows the links between maps (edges, doors, caves, stairs and, with the passes, the Seagallop ferry) and points at the next door or edge, or at the person or event once you are on the objective's map. Hidden in battles, menus and messages.
- "Arrow on screen" switch in the Journey panel and an "Arrow to the objective" option; the page arrows moved to the top of the list.

## 0.1.0

- First version: Next step and Journey panels for Trainer Desk.
- Story (28 steps and the optional Sevii trip), HMs and key items, obstacles, post-game and Pokédex rewards, read from the save's flags, badges, bag, Pokédex and party.
- Upcoming steps hidden until you turn on "Show upcoming steps".
- English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese.
