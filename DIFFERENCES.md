# Differences from the unmodded game

Trainer Journey is a guide. It reads your progress and tells you where to go; it does not change the game.

## Game data and rules

Nothing. No species, moves, items, maps, text, trainers, encounters, events or battle rules are added or changed. No event flag is ever set or cleared.

## What the unmodded game does not show you

- The next step of the story, and where it happens.
- Where every HM and key item is, and what still blocks each obstacle.
- Upcoming story steps, if you turn on **Show upcoming steps**. They are hidden by default.

## Screen

- An arrow with a step count at the border of the game picture, pointing to the current objective. It hides in battles, menus and messages, and can be turned off in the mod's options.
- Two panels in Trainer Desk: **Next step** and **Journey**.

## Input

Untouched.

## Saves

The save is only read (event flags, badges, bag, Pokédex, party). The spoiler switch and the goal you chose to follow are kept in the mod's own cache.

## Turning it off

Disable the mod and the arrow and panels are gone. Nothing is left in the save.
