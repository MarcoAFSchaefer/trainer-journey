# Trainer Journey: player guide

## Setting up

1. Install Trainer Desk and Trainer Journey (copy both folders into `%APPDATA%\pokemon-love2d\mods`).
2. Start or load a FireRed or LeafGreen game. Two panels appear in the docks: **Next step** (on the right) and **Journey** (at the bottom). Drag their tabs to place them wherever you like, as with any Trainer Desk panel.
3. Pick the language in the mod's options (**Language**).

## Next step

The top line is your current objective, followed by where to go and what helps there. Under it:

- a bar with the steps of the journey done so far (story and post-game);
- **On the way**: things you can do right now besides the objective:
  - a gift from one of Oak's aides that you have caught enough kinds for;
  - an HM you have, with the badge to use it, that nobody in your party knows yet;
  - items you can pick up in places you have already reached;
  - an optional side trip that is open.

When there are more than fit, the last line says how many more are in the Journey panel.

## Journey

Five tabs at the top:

| Tab | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| Story | Every step of the main story. A green check is done, the filled blue mark is the current step, an outlined mark is open (optional side trips). |
| HMs & items | Where each HM and key item is and whether you have it. For HMs you have: whether it works outside battle with your badges and whether someone in the party knows it. |
| Obstacles | What blocks the way and what is still missing for each one ("Needs: HM03 Surf, Soul Badge"). |
| Post-game | After the Hall of Fame: the National Pokédex, Celio's network, Mewtwo and the legendary birds. |
| Pokédex | Oak's aides' gifts and the National Pokédex, with how many kinds you still need. |

Click a line to read it in full in the box below the list. The mouse wheel, or the arrows, turn the pages.

### Spoilers

With **Show upcoming steps** off (the default), entries you have not reached yet are not named: the list ends with "N more steps to come". Turn the switch on to see everything; turn it off again whenever you like. The current objective is always shown.

## The arrow

While you walk, an arrow at the border of the game screen points the way to the current objective, with the number of steps to the point it shows:

- on another map: the door, cave, stair or map edge to take next on the way;
- on the objective's map: the person to talk to or the event (when the guide knows the spot), as a marker bobbing over it when it is on screen;
- at a harbor, when the way goes by sea: "Take the Seagallop ferry".

It counts steps in a straight line, so ledges, trees and walls can make the real walk longer. It is hidden in battles, menus, messages and cutscenes. Turn it on or off with the **Arrow on screen** switch at the bottom of the Journey panel, or the **Arrow to the objective** option.

## A goal of your own

The arrow normally follows the story. To go after something else, open the Journey panel, click its line (HM03 Surf in "HMs & items", an aide's gift in "Pokédex", a legendary in "Post-game"...) and press **Guide me here** in the box below the list. The line gets a "MY GOAL" mark, the arrow leads to it, and the Next step panel shows it with the story's next step underneath.

The goal is dropped when you get the thing, or when you press **Stop guiding**. Lines hidden as spoilers cannot be chosen until you show upcoming steps.

## How it knows

The guide reads the same flags the game uses for its story (badges, gifts received, people rescued, places visited), the key items in your bag, the Pokédex count and your party's moves. It never writes anything.
